# Design Notes

## Direction to explore

**Playful specialty coffee.**

The visual system should combine strong black outlines, warm cream space, controlled accent colours, rounded shapes and small illustrated elements.

The goal is not to make every surface decorative. The strongest visual moments should appear around brand storytelling, product discovery and interaction feedback.

## Guardrails

- Body text remains highly readable.
- Colour is used for hierarchy, not noise.
- Illustrations support content.
- Rounded controls remain recognizable as controls.
- Motion is subtle and purposeful.
- Product photography remains credible.
- Desktop and mobile must share the same hierarchy.

## Open questions
